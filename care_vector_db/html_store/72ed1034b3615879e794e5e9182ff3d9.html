You can hand wash Pixel 4a, Pixel 4a (5G), and Pixel 5 fabric cases. If needed, first use a stain remover pen or a cotton swab soaked in isopropyl alcohol on any stains. Avoid harsh cleaners. Do not scrub or soak your case.

You can only machine-wash Pixel 4a, Pixel 4a (5G), and Pixel 5 fabric cases. Do not machine-wash newer case models including recycled polycarbonate and aluminum cases.

  1. Put your case in the washer.

To treat a stain, run the stain under cold water. You can use stain remover, a stain remover pen, or a cotton swab soaked in isopropyl alcohol on the stain. Avoid harsh cleaners. Do not scrub or soak your case.
