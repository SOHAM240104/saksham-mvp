Thread-based accessories require a Thread-enabled home hub such as Apple TV 4K, HomePod (2nd generation), HomePod mini, or a compatible third-party Thread border router. are able to locally pair and manage Thread accessories, which may require an update from the accessory manufacturer.

The Home app lets you easily navigate and control your accessories. In the Home app, tap the Home tab to show the following sections:

  • Tap a category such as Climate, Lights, Security, Speakers & TVs, or Water to show all related accessories on one screen, organized by room.

  • Create scenes to control multiple accessories with a tap or Siri request—tap a scene to close the blinds and turn on the lights in the evening, for example.

  • To change the way sections such as Favorites or Scenes are ordered on the Home tab, choose Reorder Sections, then drag next to a section to a new position. Tap Done when you’re finished.

To learn more about how to create and accessorize a smart home with your Apple devices, tap Discover at the bottom of the screen.
