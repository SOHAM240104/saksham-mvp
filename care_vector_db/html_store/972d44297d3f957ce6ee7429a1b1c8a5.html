    Add more homes with iPhone

    In the Home app, you can add more than one physical space—a home and a small office, for example.

    1. Go to the Home app on your iPhone.

    2. Tap , then tap Add New Home.

    3. Name the home, choose its wallpaper, then tap Save.

    4. To switch to another home, tap , then tap the home you want.
