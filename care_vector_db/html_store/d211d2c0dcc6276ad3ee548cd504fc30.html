You'll sometimes get animations in the search bar on your Home screen. The animations are temporary for special events, like holidays.

At the bottom of your screen is a row of your favorite apps. Your phone suggests apps for your favorites based on your recent and most-used apps, as well as your routines. Your suggested apps have a colored shadow around the icon.

When there’s an empty space in the row, a new suggested app will appear. You can pin, remove, or add any of the suggested apps. You can also manually swap out any app.
