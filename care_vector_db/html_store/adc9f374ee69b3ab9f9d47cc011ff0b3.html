You can reflect on how you’re feeling and log your momentary emotions and daily moods to keep track of your mental wellbeing. You can also learn how your state of mind may correlate with lifestyle factors like exercise, sleep, time in daylight, and mindful minutes.

  1. To log your state of mind for a previous day, tap at the top-right corner, tap a day, then tap Log.

    • Tap an option from the time range selections at the top of the chart.

    • Tap States, then tap Daily Moods or Momentary Emotions to see those entries on the graph.

    • Tap Associations, then scroll through the different factors you logged in your state of mind entries.

    • Tap Life Factors, then tap a lifestyle factor to see it alongside your state of mind entries.
