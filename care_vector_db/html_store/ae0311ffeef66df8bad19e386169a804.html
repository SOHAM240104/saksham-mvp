You can browse the web, view websites, preview website links, translate webpages, and add the Safari app back to your Home Screen if it gets removed. Sign in to iCloud with the same on multiple devices to keep your open tabs, bookmarks, history, and Reading List up to date across all your devices.

    • Double-tap the top edge of the screen to quickly return to the top of a long page.

When you view a webpage or image that’s in another language, you can use Safari to translate the text (not available in all languages or regions).

If you don’t see Safari on your Home Screen, you can find it in App Library and add it back.
